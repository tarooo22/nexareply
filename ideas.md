# NexaReply visual direction

NexaReply is a **Georgian-first AI commerce assistant**. The visual language combines bold Georgian display typography, Messenger-inspired rounded conversation geometry, and a trustworthy operations dashboard.

| Design element | Direction |
|---|---|
| Primary identity | Deep violet signals AI assistance and primary action. |
| Verified state | Pale cyan represents catalog-backed, reviewed, or demo-safe automation. |
| Sales meaning | Green marks a confirmed/healthy state; amber marks review; red marks escalation. |
| Product motif | Rounded message shapes, threaded conversation rhythm, and compact ticket/status markers. |
| Tone | Specific, operational, and commerce-aware rather than generic SaaS language. |

The public site and Demo workspace should share the same violet/cyan identity, high-contrast Georgian headlines, and concise workflow copy. New components should avoid generic card stacking when a conversation, status, or workflow-specific surface communicates the information more clearly.
